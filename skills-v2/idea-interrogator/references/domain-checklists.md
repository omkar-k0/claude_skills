# Domain question banks

Starting points for the interrogation round. Adapt to the specific idea, do not read them out as a list. Pick the 3 to 5 that hit hardest.

## Trading / quant ideas
- What is the exact, falsifiable edge in one sentence? (If the answer describes a pattern without a reason it exists, flag it.)
- How many independent trades would the backtest produce? Under ~100, statistical significance is off the table.
- What does this idea assume about fills, spread, and slippage, and has that assumption been tested at the worst hour of the session?
- If entries were replaced with random entries using the same exit logic, what do you expect happens? Would you bet on it?
- Who is on the other side of this trade and why are they wrong?
- What is the max drawdown you would actually sit through before abandoning the system live?

## Career / job / Werkstudent ideas
- What does the job market say, today, about demand for this exact role in Germany? (Verify live.)
- Which hard requirement in real postings do you currently fail? (German level, certification, years of experience.)
- What is the opportunity cost: what are you NOT doing during the months this takes?
- Who has actually made this exact transition, and did they start from your position or a better one?
- Does this move survive if the Werkstudent/visa hour constraints are enforced strictly?
- Is this idea pulling you toward something or away from something? (Escape ideas fail differently than ambition ideas.)

## Purchases (bikes, gear, tech, courses)
- Cost per use: honest estimate of uses in the first 90 days, divided into price.
- What do you already own that does 80% of this?
- Is the used market (Kleinanzeigen) price checked, live, today?
- What is the resale value if this turns out to be a mistake?
- Is this purchase solving the problem, or is it a substitute for the boring action that actually solves it? (Buying gear instead of training.)

## Projects / builds / side projects
- What is the smallest version that produces real feedback, and why aren't you building that instead?
- Who is user number one, by name? "People like me" is not an answer.
- What is the maintenance cost after it exists? Dead projects with your name on them are a cost.
- Does this compete with an existing free tool? Name it. Why does yours exist?
- What has to be true about your weekly schedule for this to survive exam season?

## Academic ideas (thesis topics, methods, courses)
- Does the data for this actually exist, and do you have access rights to it? (Entitlement failures killed work before; verify first.)
- What would the professor's harshest question be, and what is your answer today?
- Is the method within what you can execute solo in the timeframe, or does it assume skills you plan to learn along the way?
- What is the shortest path to a passing deliverable, and how far does this idea deviate from it?

## Travel / trip ideas
- Is it Deutschland-Ticket valid end to end, verified against live schedules?
- What is the total honest cost including food, local transit, and the thing you'll impulse-buy?
- What is the plan when the last regional connection is missed?
- Is there footage/content value, or is that a rationalization for the cost?

## Health / training ideas
- Does this violate any recovery rule (consecutive climbing days, volume + intensity jumps in the same week)?
- What does the actual training log from the last two weeks say about readiness?
- Is this sustainable for 8 weeks, or is it a two-week enthusiasm spike?
- What is the injury cost if it goes wrong, in weeks lost?

## Fallback (uncategorized ideas)
- What has to be true for this to work? List it, then check each item.
- What is the base rate of success for people attempting this?
- If a friend brought you this idea, what would you tell him?
- What is the cost of being wrong, and is it recoverable?
